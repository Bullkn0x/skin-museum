Curator's desk

In-game pictures

The museum shows Steam's stock render for an exhibit until you give it a picture of the real item, with your stickers, wear and pattern. A picture with a transparent background floats in its display; a normal screenshot is hung as a framed photograph.

  1. Get a picture of the item. Either take a screenshot while inspecting it in CS2, or copy its inspect link below and paste it into a screenshot tool such as swap.gg/screenshot or csmarketcap.com/screenshot.
  2. Save it in the project's assets/ingame/ folder under the file name shown for that exhibit (.png, .jpg or .webp all work).
  3. Run node scripts/sync-skins.mjs --images, then reload the museum.
